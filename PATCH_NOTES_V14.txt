SCAG V14 — RESUMABLE TRANSCRIPTION + LIVE CONSOLE LOGS

1. Entire-stream Whisper transcription is now executed in 5-minute chunks.
2. Absolute timestamps are restored, so the final transcript remains one complete
   timestamped stream transcript.
3. Each completed chunk is checkpointed under work/transcript_chunks/.
4. work/transcript_partial.json is updated after every completed chunk.
5. If a run is interrupted, completed chunks are reused on the next run.
6. Streamlit now shows transcription chunk progress instead of sitting on "Preparing...".
7. Streamlit mirrors child-process logs to the VS Code/PowerShell console in real time.
8. PYTHONUNBUFFERED is enabled for the child process so logs are not held in buffers.
9. The full transcript is still passed to Qwen. Candidate discovery/editorial architecture
   is unchanged.
10. Source video quality/download settings are unchanged.
